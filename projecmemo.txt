# Problem memo -- Koba Cage (Zoe Ebert, Gerrit Ewing)

## The user
Gerrit and I’s mutual friend, Rose, and her crested gecko Koba.

## The problem
In order to mimic the crested geckos environment in New Caledonia, Rose must
mist her gecko’s cage twice a day in order to keep it at 80% to 90% humidity
during those periods. However ideally she would like it to be at 55%— this is hard
for her when traditional and less expensive automatic terrarium misters
generally are timer-based and not based on the recorded humidity. Rose’s
situation is specifically unideal as she has opted to mist it manually, which means
she cannot make sure the conditions are accurate. We will also implement a heat
sensor to monitor the heat and keep it at the ideal levels, overall mimicking the
environment most suited for Koba.

## Why
Because the humidity and heat must always be kept up, especially in a dryer
climate like Colorado, 24/7 sensing must be offered in order to mimic the gecko’s
ideal environment. Additionally because there are quite a few moving parts,
between the sensors and heater, as well as mister, it is likely that any sort of
‘phone app’ or package offer for a service like this would be quite expensive.

## The sensors
HT45 Temperature and Humidity Sensor and DS18B20 Temperature Sensor
Probe. These sensors will work together and somewhat fused, as both measures
temperature (as well as one measuring humidity). Temperature and humidity
will come together for the ultimate goal of creating the idea climate for Koba, but
additionally we will likely situate the sensors on opposite sides of his cage in
order to use the temperature sensors to their full ability.

## The mechanisms 
Kernel module/character driver: We will use a sensor driver to allow our system
to communicate with the hardware, our temperature and humidity sensors.
Real-time scheduling: I believe we will likely need to prioritize certain processes’
over others, especially if Koba’s continued comfort and safety is our goal.

## The risk
Likely the single biggest risk of this project is the outcome if we fail— we will do
everything in advance to ensure that the project is safe to be set up in Koba’s
cage beforehand but all our code needs to be airtight so we do not end up hurting
him. Of course, we will not have this assignment set up in his cage to begin with
at all— the vast majority of this project will be done somewhere else.
